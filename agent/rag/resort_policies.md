## 3. Extra Guest Policy and Additional Charges

1. **Extra Guest Fee:** 
   - The fee for an additional guest is **PHP 1,500 per person per night**.
   - This fee covers a rollaway bed or mattress setup, complete linen and towel set, toiletries kit, and access to resort facilities.
   - For room types where breakfast is included (Family Room, Oceanfront Villa, Presidential Suite), the extra guest fee **includes daily breakfast buffet**.
2. **Occupancy Limits:** 
   - Extra guests are strictly permitted only up to the maximum occupancy defined for each room type:
     - Standard Room: Max 1 extra guest (Total 3)
     - Deluxe Room: Max 1 extra guest (Total 5)
     - Family Room: Max 2 extra guests (Total 8)
     - Oceanfront Pool Villa: Max 1 extra guest (Total 5)
     - Presidential Suite: Max 2 extra guests (Total 6)
3. **Children Policy:**
   - Children aged **0 to 5 years old** stay **free of charge** when sharing existing bedding with parents (maximum 2 free children per room).
   - Children aged **6 to 11 years old** sharing existing bedding are charged **PHP 500 per night** (covers breakfast buffet and facility access; no separate bed).
   - Children aged **12 years and above** are classified as adults and require the standard **PHP 1,500 per night extra guest charge**.

## 12. Cancellation and Refund Policy

### Standard Bookings
- **Cancellation 7 or more days prior to check-in:** 100% full refund of the deposit paid (less a 5% administrative payment-processing fee).
- **Cancellation 3 to 6 days prior to check-in:** 50% refund of the deposit paid.
- **Cancellation less than 3 days prior to check-in:** Non-refundable.
- **No-Show:** 100% forfeit of total deposit.

### Non-Refundable Promotional Bookings
- Reservations booked under special promotional discount codes marked "Non-Refundable" cannot be cancelled or refunded under any circumstances. Date changes are permitted once with a PHP 1,000 rebooking fee, subject to rate differences.

### Resort-Initiated / Safety Cancellations
- If the resort cancels reservations due to severe force majeure, structural repairs, or government-mandated island closures, the guest is offered:
  1. Full 100% immediate refund with no administrative deduction, or
  2. An open voucher valid for 12 months with a complimentary one-category room upgrade.

## 13. Weather, Marine, and Force Majeure Policy

The resort is situated in a tropical coastal zone. During inclement weather, heavy swells, tropical storm warnings, or Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) sea travel advisories:
- The resort may suspend boat transfers, water sports, island hopping, and outdoor swimming.
- If a guest is unable to arrive on the island due to official Coast Guard boat cancellations, the resort will reschedule the stay without penalty or provide a full refund for the affected night(s).
- If sea travel is suspended while guests are already on the island, extended accommodation for stranded guests is provided at a **30% discount** off standard room rates.

## 14. Environmental, Marine Conservation, and Local Fees

1. **Municipal Environmental User Fee (EUF):**
   - The local government levies an environmental fee of **PHP 150 per tourist** upon arrival at the port terminal. This fee supports coral reef conservation and local waste management.
2. **Marine Sanctuary Rules:**
   - Touching, stepping on, or collecting corals, starfish, shells, and marine wildlife is strictly prohibited by national law. Violations carry fines up to PHP 10,000.
   - Only **reef-safe mineral sunscreens** (free of oxybenzone and octinoxate) are permitted when swimming in resort marine zones. Reef-safe sunscreen is available for purchase at the Resort Shop.
3. **Single-Use Plastics:**
   - Single-use plastic water bottles and plastic shopping bags are prohibited on resort grounds. Complimentary refillable glass water bottles and water stations are provided on every floor.

## 15. House Rules, Safety, and Guest Conduct

- **Pet Policy:** Pets are generally not permitted inside guest rooms or dining facilities. Certified service guide animals are welcomed free of charge with prior notification.
- **Smoking Policy:** Smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited in all guest rooms, balconies, restaurants, and indoor venues. Designated outdoor smoking pavilions are located near the South Garden. A **PHP 5,000 deep-cleaning fee** will be levied for smoking inside guest rooms.
- **Quiet Hours:** Observed nightly from **10:00 PM to 7:00 AM**. Noise levels must be kept to a minimum in all corridors, balconies, and pool areas.
- **Alcohol & Illegal Substances:** Bringing illegal drugs or firearms onto resort premises results in immediate eviction without refund and notification of law enforcement.
- **Pool Safety:** Swimming pools operate from **7:00 AM to 9:00 PM**. No diving is permitted. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Glassware is prohibited in pool zones.
- **Lost and Found:** Surrendered items are cataloged and kept at the Front Desk for **60 days**. Return courier shipping can be arranged at the guest's expense.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the extra guest policy and charge?
The extra guest fee is **PHP 1,500 per person per night**. It covers extra bedding setup, full towel and toiletry sets, facility access, and breakfast buffet (for rooms with breakfast included). Extra guests are permitted up to the room's maximum occupancy limit.

### How much is the cheapest room?
The Standard Room is the lowest-priced room at **PHP 2,500 per night** for 2 guests.

### What are the room rates across categories?
- Standard Room: PHP 2,500/night (2 pax)
- Deluxe Room: PHP 4,500/night (4 pax)
- Family Room: PHP 6,000/night (6 pax, includes breakfast)
- Oceanfront Pool Villa: PHP 12,000/night (4 pax, private pool, butler, transfers, breakfast)
- Presidential Suite: PHP 18,000/night (4 pax, jacuzzi, panoramic terrace, breakfast)

### Can I bring outside food and drinks?
Packaged dry snacks and non-perishables are allowed. Cooked dishes and outside catering are prohibited. Corkage fees apply for alcohol: PHP 500 per wine bottle and PHP 1,000 per liquor bottle.

### How much are boat transfers?
Scheduled shared boat transfers cost **PHP 200 per person** each way. Private chartered boat transfers cost **PHP 2,500 per boat** (up to 8 passengers).

### What time are check-in and check-out?
Standard check-in is **2:00 PM** and check-out is **12:00 PM**. Early check-in (before 10 AM) costs 50% of the room rate; late checkout is PHP 500/hour up to 4:00 PM.
