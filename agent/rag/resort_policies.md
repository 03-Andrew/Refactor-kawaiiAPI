## 6. Check-In and Check-Out

- Early check-in is subject to room availability.
- A valid government-issued identification document may be requested at check-in.
- The resort may require a refundable security deposit at check-in.

## 9. Cancellation and Refund Policy

### Standard Cancellation

For standard bookings:

- Cancellation **7 or more days before check-in:** eligible for a full refund of the refundable amount.
- Cancellation **3–6 days before check-in:** eligible for a 50% refund of the refundable amount.
- Cancellation **less than 3 days before check-in:** generally non-refundable.
- No-show reservations are non-refundable unless an exception is approved by management.

Refund processing may take several business days depending on the original payment method.

### Non-Refundable Reservations

Bookings explicitly marked as **non-refundable** cannot be refunded after confirmation. Changes may also be restricted.

### Resort-Initiated Cancellation

If the resort cancels a booking because the property cannot safely accommodate guests, the guest may choose between:

1. Rescheduling to an available date, or
2. Receiving a refund for the affected booking.
